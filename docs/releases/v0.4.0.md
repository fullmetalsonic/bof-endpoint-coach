- 200% 상당 화면 재배치와 모달 Escape·포커스 순환·복귀

## English

v0.4.0 combines the storage/recovery P0 findings and related usability P1 findings from the extended v0.3.0 audit.

- Atomic storage-revision conflict protection across multiple HTML windows.
- Fail-closed IndexedDB loading, save retry, emergency backup, and unload warning.
- Recoverable local drafts for heat, event, stage, correction, and settings forms.
- Reasoned settings revisions with actor, time, previous version, and field-level changes.
- Active-heat freshness/check summaries and searchable, filterable, sortable, paged history.
- Reflow through a 960×540 viewport and accessible modal focus/Escape behavior.
- Read-verification of each backup ZIP before download.
- Clearer DEMO-data, DEMO-reference, literature, override, and approval boundaries.

Plant endpoint accuracy remains unvalidated. This candidate does not replace equipment control, safety interlocks, SOPs, tap authorization, laboratory results, or operator judgment.
